## Summary

Research engineer and AI scientist focused on machine learning systems for biomedical data, with experience spanning clinical AI, predictive modeling, multimodal learning, and deployable research software. Current work at INRIA centers on small foundation models, representation learning, and practical model design for biology data. Background includes building real-world ICU decision-support tools, working with de-identified clinical datasets, and translating methods into usable software artifacts for healthcare settings.
